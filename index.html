Three stories. 45 seconds.

Sharjah

A destination worth discovering.

voco

04

Our idea

Two ways to get there. One place to be.

Two professionals travel to the same important meeting. Colleagues — but completely different personalities, and completely different ways of travelling.

Reveals Sharjah

The Creative Director

  • Curious
  • Informal
  • Spontaneous

Backpack. Headphones. Sneakers.

Uses the time before the meeting to go out and experience Sharjah.

Reveals voco

The Accounts Director

  • Precise
  • Structured
  • Considered

Tailored clothes. Leather bag. Schedule-driven.

Uses the same hours to enjoy the hospitality, wellness and social spaces of voco.

Their journeys converge at Thara.

  • Creative DirectorReveals Sharjah
  • Accounts DirectorReveals voco
  • Both togetherReveal Thara

Neither is presented as better, more successful or more professional than the other. They are equals with different personalities.

05

Cinematic language

Fast. Precise. Character-driven.

The pace, visual confidence and playful precision associated with Guy Ritchie–style filmmaking — held at a level that stays premium and appropriate for voco, Thara and Sharjah.

Not an imitation of one film, shot for shot. The reference is the broader cinematographic language: rhythm, symmetry and visual wit.

  • Split screens
  • Rapid intercutting
  • Fast editorial rhythm
  • Snap zooms
  • Controlled punch-ins
  • Whip pans
  • Match cuts
  • Graphic transitions
  • Selective speed ramps
  • Tight inserts
  • Macro details
  • Parallel action
  • Visual symmetry
  • Dynamic camera
  • Unexpected transitions
  • Sound-led editing
  • Repetition
  • Callbacks

The two-character structure drives the camera

  • Backpack zipper→Leather bag buckle
  • Bike wheel spinning→Aircraft turbine
  • Coffee cup on table→Passport on counter
  • Elevator doors close→Thara doors open
  • Headphones on→Cuff adjusted
  • One notification→Two phones, split screen
Energy→Contrast→Convergence→Tension→Release

Fast, clever and energetic at the top. More controlled and cinematic as the meeting approaches — the meeting is the climax. After the deal closes, the pace relaxes into warmer, human moments. Despite the speed, every shot stays premium and beautifully composed.

06

Synopsis

One meeting. Two journeys.

I

Two worlds

Two completely different environments. The Creative Director cycling through the city. The Accounts Director at an elegant dinner. Their phones vibrate. Split screen. The same meeting notification arrives — Sharjah. Tomorrow.

II

The journey

The film accelerates. Passport, bike lock, leather bag, backpack zipper, boarding pass, security, headphones, watch, aircraft, landing. The two journeys constantly echo each other through match cuts.

III

Four hours

They land in Sharjah and arrive at voco. Warm welcome. Check-in. Rooms. 4 hours until the meeting. Their paths separate — one heads into the city for architecture, coffee, people and culture; the other settles into voco, the pool, the gym, the lobby, and the people in it.

IV

Convergence

The edit tightens. Watch. Taxi. Elevator. Laptop. Shoes walking. Meeting room. The doors open and they see each other. A small moment — “Ready?” They enter Thara. The Creative Director presents, the Accounts Director supports. A pause. A look across the table. Handshake. The deal is done.

V

Release

That evening, both sides around the same dinner table. Jackets off. Food, conversation, laughter. Business has become connection. Finally, the Creative Director falls backwards onto his bed. Match cut. The Accounts Director falls backwards onto his. Same framing. Silence.
